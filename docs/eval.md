# Evaluation

The hero task, learned once from a recorded demo, then run on every email. Generated by `node backend/test/eval.ts` on 2026-10-30. Written from the run's saved data after the evaluation was stopped (low memory) before it could write its report, so model calls and the store's state afterwards weren't captured.

**The task.** "Resolve a complaint": Resolve each customer complaint by checking the order and parcel status then replacing, refunding or declining with a reply. Learned on 2026-10-30 from one demo (one complaint handled across the inbox, the store and the courier tracker, rules said out loud). 4 rules, 14 of 14 built-in checks pass against the demo.

**The runs.** All 15 seeded emails, run without stopping for approvals (so a wrong decision would really act), on the store as recorded and on a redesigned store (different labels and layout). Questions were answered the way a person would.

| Time, all emails | 213 s | 418 s |

How it is scored: a decision is right when it matches the test data's answer; for the two emails outside the rules (a wrong item sent) asking you, or reusing your answer, is right. A refund for a lost parcel counts whatever the learned task named the outcome (it may use one refund outcome for both; the store action is the same). Your answers were given the way a person would, so an email Remuse asked about counts as right; "Decided alone" shows the decisions it made without you.

**Practice** (what happens right after learning): the demo email wasn't found in the list, so it practiced on the first one: found 8 of 8 values, chose send_replacement, stopped before "Send replacement".
